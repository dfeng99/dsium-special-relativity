## Paper

This standalone paper proposes an algebraic-projection interpretation of Special Relativity within the Discrete & Shapeless Information Universe Model (DSIUM). It treats the invariant system stride limit as an underlying structural constraint and examines familiar relativistic effects as macroscopic coordinate projections.

The manuscript includes SageMath numerical simulations in Appendix A and discusses the Hecke-orbit construction in Appendix B.
